About this plannerSource: CPT BAT Courses Offered 2026–2027 Catalog.xlsx. Program courses are the black X courses and electives the blue X courses in the “CPT Program Courses” and “BAT Program Courses” tabs; credits and prerequisites from the “CPT” and “BAT” tabs. Completion is the share of program courses checked off (plus the chosen specialization’s courses for Engineering Technology). The sheet does not say how many electives each program requires, so electives are counted separately. General education courses are not in the sheet and are not included. Overlap bars show the share of each other program’s required courses covered. Internship credits vary and are counted at the low end of the range. Hover a course to see its prerequisites and the programs it belongs to. Checkmarks are saved only in this browser and carry over when you switch programs.
